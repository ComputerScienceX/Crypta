# Privacy Policy for Crypta (EncryptedMessenger)

Effective date: April 29, 2026

Crypta ("we", "our", "us") provides secure messaging features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Information We Collect
- Account information: email/username and profile details.
- User content: messages, media, and files you choose to send.
- Device/app data: device type, OS version, app version, crash/performance logs.
- Optional location data: only if you enable location features.
- Purchase/subscription data: status and entitlements (processed by Apple).

## How We Use Information
- Provide and operate messaging features.
- Authenticate users and secure accounts.
- Deliver core app functions (including encrypted communication).
- Improve reliability, performance, and abuse prevention.
- Provide support and respond to requests.

## Encryption
Crypta is designed to use end-to-end encryption for supported communications. We cannot read message content in transit where E2EE is enabled.

## Sharing
We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with:
- Service providers (hosting, analytics, crash reporting, support tools).
- Payment providers (Apple) for subscription processing.
- Law enforcement when required by law.

## Data Retention
We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## Your Choices
You can:
- Update profile/account details in-app.
- Control permissions (camera, photos, microphone, location) in iOS Settings.
- Request account deletion by contacting support.

## Children’s Privacy
Crypta is not intended for children under 13 (or higher minimum age where required by local law).

## International Transfers
Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, with safeguards where required.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above.
